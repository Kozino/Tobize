The Flagship

Where Great Food Meets Great Memories

Authentic African & continental cuisine under one roof — from charcoal grills to a dessert counter worth saving room for.

Tobize Intercontinental is the newest chapter of the Tobize family: a modern restaurant at Bin Twar Center, Freej Kulaib (opposite Al Jazeera Television). Start with calamari & shrimp, move on to lamb chops on saffron rice or our signature grilled farrouj, then finish with garri parfait or a crème brûlée. It's Doha on a plate — African soul, international flair.
