Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the two Tobize restaurants the same company?
Yes — Tobize African Restaurant (Najma) and Tobize Intercontinental Restaurant (Freej Kulaib) are owned by the same person/family. They share the Tobize name and hospitality, but each has its own kitchen, menu and prices.
Which one is the "green logo" and which is the red flame?
The green pot badge belongs to Tobize African Restaurant in Najma — the Nigerian home-cooking branch. The red-and-white flame logo belongs to Tobize Intercontinental in Freej Kulaib — the modern international grill & desserts branch.
Do the menus and prices differ?
Yes, they are different kitchens. The African branch focuses on Nigerian home cooking (jollof, egusi, suya, porridge, swallow — dishes from ~QAR 25). The Intercontinental serves international grills, seafood, pasta, pizza plus African specials and desserts (most dishes QAR 15–75). See each branch's page for priced highlights and menu links.
Where can I see the full menus online?
African branch: full priced menus on Talabat and Snoonu. Intercontinental: the official menu is on tobize.com/#menu.
What are your opening hours?
Both branches are open every day from 9:00 AM to 1:00 AM — breakfast through late-night cravings.
Can you cater events at either location?
Yes — party trays, egusi pots, small chops platters (up to QAR 500 at the Intercontinental) and suya platters. Message the branch you prefer on WhatsApp for a quote.
