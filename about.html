One Family, Two Kitchens

The Tobize Story

From one pot in Najma to two restaurants across Doha — the same family, the same promise: honest food, rich spices and memories worth staying for.

How It Started

First Najma, Then the World

Tobize African Restaurant — known lovingly as “the Nigeria Restaurant” — opened its doors in Najma with a simple mission: nobody in Doha should miss the taste of home-cooked African food.

The green pot logo became a beacon for the community: smoky party jollof on Sundays, suya off the grill, egusi with pounded yam, and the kind of hospitality that keeps guests at the table for hours. Word spread, reviews grew to 76 on Google, and delivery apps carried the flavour across the whole city.

Then came the next chapter. The same family lit a new flame — Tobize Intercontinental Restaurant in Freej Kulaib, a modern space where the charcoal grill meets international cuisine: lamb chops, grilled farrouj, seafood, pasta, wood-fired pizza, desserts and fresh juices. Two logos, two menus, one family. Wherever you choose to sit, you're family at Tobize.
