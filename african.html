The Nigeria Restaurant

A Taste of Home in Najma

Whether you're craving a delicious home-cooked African meal or need high-quality African food ingredients, Tobize African has you covered.

Known locally as “the Nigeria Restaurant”, our Najma kitchen cooks the classics the proper way — smoky party jollof, peppered Isiewu, egu.si simmered with assorted proteins, and suya crusted in yaji spice. A favourite of Doha's Nigerian community, rated 4.4★ by 76 Google reviewers.
